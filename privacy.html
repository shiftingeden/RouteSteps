RouteSteps Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

RouteSteps is made by TechBitsMix (Fredy Soussou), an independent developer in Quebec, Canada. In short: RouteSteps does not collect any data.

What the app uses on your iPhone

  • Location (GPS) — only while you are recording a walk, to draw your path on a map. Location updates stop when you tap Stop.
  • Location (step zones, Pro, optional) — if you create a step zone, iOS tells RouteSteps when you arrive at or leave that circle, even when the app is closed (this needs “Always” location access). Only the arrival and departure times are kept, to count your steps inside the zone. While you are inside a zone, low-accuracy location (about 100 m, mostly Wi-Fi) keeps the step count on your Lock Screen up to date and notices when you leave; those positions are not stored, and your location is never sent to us. Zones and visits sync only through your own private iCloud.
  • Motion & Fitness — step counts, floors climbed and distance from your iPhone's motion chip, and height from the barometer.
  • Body weight — a number you enter in Settings, used only to estimate calories.
  • Apple Health (optional) — if you turn on “Save walks to Apple Health”, each walk is written to Health as a Walking workout with its path and estimated active calories. Steps, distance and floors are not written, so they are never counted twice. RouteSteps only writes to Health; it never reads your Health data. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.
  • Apple Watch (optional) — the Watch app records walks with the watch's sensors and sends them directly to your iPhone. It uses a workout session only to keep recording; nothing is saved to Health from the watch.

Your walks, paths and route names are stored on your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, synced to your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit so you keep your history when you reinstall or change iPhone. Only you can access that iCloud data; it is never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Purchases

The optional RouteSteps Pro purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Retention and deleting your data

Walks, paths and routes are kept until you delete them. Delete individual walks or routes in the app; deletions also apply to your iCloud copy. To remove everything, delete the walks in the app, or remove RouteSteps data in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage. Location and motion readings are only used while a walk is being recorded, or to count steps inside a step zone you created, and are not kept anywhere else. Deleting a zone deletes its history. Walks you chose to save to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there.

Children

RouteSteps does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
