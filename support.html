Frequently asked

The map says "No GPS path". GPS needs a view of the sky, so indoors (offices, malls, metro stations) there is usually no path. Steps, floors and height climbed still count.

Why is "Floors" 0 after taking stairs? iPhone counts a floor as about 3 m (10 ft) of climbing. Shorter staircases show up under "Climbed" instead.

How are calories estimated? About 0.5 kcal per kg of body weight per km walked, plus the extra work of climbing. Set your weight in Settings. It's an estimate, not medical advice.

Can I record with the phone locked? Yes. Keep walking normally; the step count for the exact start-to-stop window is fetched when you tap Stop.

I bought Pro on another device. Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases.
